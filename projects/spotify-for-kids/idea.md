# Project: Spotify for Kids

## Product Overview
Build a streaming music and audiobook platform designed specifically for children ages 5-10. The platform will provide age-appropriate songs, stories, and educational audio content in a safe, ad-free environment with parental controls.

---

## Context

### Business Goal
- Create a dedicated kids' entertainment platform that parents trust
- Capture the growing market for child-safe digital content
- Build recurring subscription revenue from families
- Establish brand loyalty with families during formative years

### Target Users
**Primary Users (Children)**:
- Kids aged 5-10 years old
- Various interests: music, stories, learning
- Different reading/listening levels
- Mix of independent and guided usage

**Secondary Users (Parents)**:
- Want safe, age-appropriate content for their children
- Concerned about screen time and content quality
- Need visibility and control over what kids access
- Willing to pay for premium, ad-free experiences

### Technical Constraints
- Must work on web (desktop/tablet) and mobile apps (iOS/Android)
- Content delivery via CDN for global reach
- Offline download capability for travel/limited connectivity
- Low latency streaming (<2s buffering)
- Parental controls must be secure (PIN-protected)
- COPPA compliant (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

### Success Metrics
- 10,000 active users within 3 months of launch
- 70% weekly active users (WAU/MAU ratio)
- Average 30 minutes listening time per day per user
- 60% conversion from free trial to paid subscription
- <5% churn rate monthly
- 4.5+ star rating in app stores

### Known Assumptions
- Parents are willing to pay $9.99/month for ad-free kids content
- Kids aged 5-10 can navigate simple interfaces independently
- Age-based content filtering is sufficient (vs. individual customization)
- Audio content is preferred over video for this age group
- English-language content is sufficient for MVP (expand to other languages later)

### Out of Scope (for MVP)
- Live streaming or radio features
- Social features (friend lists, sharing, commenting)
- User-generated content
- Video content
- In-app purchases beyond subscription
- Multi-language support (English only for MVP)
- Web accessibility features beyond basic standards
- Integration with smart speakers (Alexa, Google Home)

### Key Features (Initial Thoughts)
- Curated library of age-appropriate songs and audiobooks
- Age-based content filtering (5-7 years, 8-10 years)
- Kid-friendly interface with large buttons and simple navigation
- Parental dashboard for monitoring and controls
- Playlist creation (both curated and custom)
- Sleep timer for bedtime stories
- No ads, no external links
- Content categories: Music, Stories, Educational

### Potential Challenges
- Content licensing costs (music rights, story rights)
- Content moderation and age-appropriateness verification
- Balancing simplicity for kids with features parents want
- Competing with free content (YouTube Kids, Spotify with family plan)
- Building sufficient content library to retain users
